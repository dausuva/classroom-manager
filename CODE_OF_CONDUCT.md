# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is incorporated here by reference.

In short: be respectful and patient, give and take criticism of the work and not of the person, and assume good faith. Harassment, insults and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable
in issues, pull requests or any other space of this project. People who work in schools and with children bring a particular responsibility: never post pupil data or pictures of pupils.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or block people who break these rules. Report a problem to support@dausuva.com; reports are handled privately.
